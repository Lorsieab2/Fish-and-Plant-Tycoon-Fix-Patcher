FISH & PLANT TYCOON FIX PATCHER

🪴 Created with Codex AI, with contributions from Claude AI. Made with love by Lorsieab2 :) 🐟

0. Use the free Windows downloads from LDW's own site, https://ldw.com.
   Those are the only supported copies. Builds from anywhere else are
   refused, not patched.
1. Close Fish Tycoon, Plant Tycoon, and any trainer before patching.
2. Double-click "Launch Fish and Plant Tycoon Fix Patcher.bat".
3. Click "Find Both in Parent Folder..." and choose the folder your game
   folders sit in. Use "Find Fish Tycoon..." or "Find Plant Tycoon..." for
   one game. You can also fill in the folders yourself.
4. Choose "One Game" or "Both Games".
5. Leave the fixes you want checked. "Defaults", "Enable All", and
   "Disable All" set the whole list for that game at once.
6. Use Dry Run to validate without writing files.
7. Choose where the modified game folders should be saved.
8. Click Create Modded Copy or Patch Both.

The patcher creates only "Fish Tycoon - Modded" and/or
"Plant Tycoon - Modded" beneath the chosen location. Their executables use
those exact names plus ".exe". With new content on (DS Exclusive Fish, DS
Store Items, Add unused flowers, or Add new store upgrades and items) the copy
is "Fish Tycoon - Modded Upgrades" / "Plant Tycoon - Modded Upgrades" instead,
so it never replaces the plain modded copy, and it keeps its own saves. The
patcher remembers the chosen paths. The blue underlined paths open those
folders in File Explorer.

About your saves: both games save to
Documents\LDW\(exe name)\(game name)(number).ldw. The folder comes from the
executable's name and the file names are the game's own. A modded copy
therefore has its own folder, for example
"Documents\LDW\Fish Tycoon - Modded\Fish Tycoon1.ldw" beside the original
"Documents\LDW\Fish Tycoon\Fish Tycoon1.ldw". Your original saves are never
touched and the two cannot overwrite each other. (Documents is often
OneDrive\Documents.)

File 0 is the settings file, not a save, and it is in the per-executable
folder too, so a modded copy starts with fresh settings. If you rename a
modded executable its saves will seem to disappear - they are still there in
the folder named after the old executable.

The original game folders are never replaced. Exact unsupported or already
modified EXEs are refused. The package contains no game executable. Its only
game files are the Plant Tycoon sounds and images the LDW version is missing,
taken from the Steam version, because LDW no longer updates the games.

To restore the original EXE inside a modded folder, choose One Game, select its
timestamped backup folder and modded output folder, then click
"Restore Output EXE from Backup".

Each option in the patcher describes exactly what it changes and where.

- Unknown Chemical: 3 uses

- Buy Multiple Golden Seahorses

  of 21. Sell or move fish down to 21 per tank before turning it off.)

  7 fins. Sell or trash every DS fish before turning it off again.)
- DS Store Items (off by default; needs DS Exclusive Fish. Adds an
  Upgrades button to the Supplies store with the DS Puffer Fish, Sting Ray,

  Trash the animals and let the eel out of
  its rock before turning it off again.)
- Time Warp (off by default; a $500 clock on the store's Upgrades screen:
  type 1-72 hours in a Windows popup and the game plays them as time away)
- Free upgrades (off by default; turns on the store's Upgrades screen:
  every item on that screen costs $0. The game's own store items keep

- Add Money cheat ($1,000) (off by default; turns on the store's Upgrades
  screen: a pile of gold coins there costs $950, or $0 with Free upgrades,

Moving things between greenhouses (5 extra greenhouses):
- A plant: drag it onto the left or right arrow, as you would drag it to
  another pot. It goes to the same pot place in that greenhouse, or to its
  first empty pot if that place is taken. If that greenhouse is full, a box
  says so and the plant stays where it was.
- Pollen: take it as usual, click an arrow, and drop it on a plant there.
  The seeds it makes are the same as within one greenhouse.
- Seeds taken from a plant: click an arrow while holding them, then plant
  them in an empty pot there, drop them on the seed box, or throw them away.
  Dropped anywhere else they go back to the plant they came from.
- Seeds from the seed tray (Legendary Seeds too), tools and chemicals stay in
  your hand when you click an arrow and work in the new greenhouse. (Changing
  to another screen, such as the store or the nursery, empties your hand as
  in the unpatched game.)
- Nursery ornaments are never placed anywhere: each counts as soon as it is
  bought, so there is nothing to move.

Getting a plant into a nursery (5 extra nurseries):
- Tag it for sale in its greenhouse as usual (select it, then the nursery
  button, or the button that tags them all). It stays in its pot.
- Tagged plants fill the nurseries in order: Nursery 1 first (a Greenhouse 1
  plant at its own pot's spot, as in the unpatched game), then Nursery 2, and
  so on, Greenhouse 1's plants before Greenhouse 2's, pot by pot. When a
  plant sells or is taken off sale, the ones after it move up.
- Use the arrows on the nursery screen to see each nursery. A nursery sells
  only while it is on screen, as the unpatched nursery sells only while you
  are in it; each has its own visitors, prices and sales.

Tools tab, Breeding Guide: pick the game, a save folder and a player. Tabs
for each part (Fish: Fins and Bodies; Plant: Flowers and Foliage), the
species with the pairs that breed them and their best prices, a Breeding
Calculator (two parents: every child and its odds) and the
Breeding Planner (the breeding chain from the species you have to the one
you want); both offer 0 effects, Max effects without the mutation, and Max
effects with it (Wasp Grouper / Mutation Liquid). It opens on the species
your save has discovered only; tick Show Spoilers under Options to see
everything. The pane under each sheet scrolls. Click a column to sort, type
in Search to filter, and Export saves the open sheet as .xlsx or .csv. It
never changes a save. Its "?" button explains the rules and what the prices
assume.
